02 / BUILDING OUR COMMUNITY

Three ways to bring
people together.

Our email strategy focuses on reaching customers through places where they already interact with The Dot Cafe. Every customer group receives a clear reason to subscribe and a long-term reason to stay connected.

01

Current In-Store Customers

Where & How We Collect

Display QR codes on tables, receipts, and signs near checkout. Employees can encourage interested customers to scan the code and complete the signup form voluntarily.

Immediate Value Exchange

Subscribers receive a proposed 10% off their next visit after confirming their email address. The offer encourages them to return to the café.

Long-Term Value Promise

Regular customers receive occasional loyalty perks, seasonal menu updates, and exclusive promotions that make staying subscribed worthwhile.

02

Iowa State College Students

Where & How We Collect

Promote the Insider Club through Instagram stories, student social media posts, and QR-code flyers around the Iowa State campus. These links direct students to the signup form.

Immediate Value Exchange

Students receive a proposed student welcome discount for a future breakfast or lunch visit after verifying their email.

Long-Term Value Promise

Subscribers stay connected through student-friendly meal promotions, group dining ideas, café updates, and occasional special offers.

03

Online Visitors & Local Followers

Where & How We Collect

Place an email signup form prominently on the website. Link to it through social media posts, local event announcements, and online café promotions.

Immediate Value Exchange

New subscribers receive a proposed 10% welcome coupon after confirming their email. This gives potential customers an incentive to visit in person.

Long-Term Value Promise

Subscribers receive updates about upcoming events, menu additions, seasonal promotions, and new ways to enjoy The Dot Cafe.

Our Permission-Based Approach: Every signup requires explicit consent. Subscribers would receive a double opt-in confirmation email before being added to the marketing list. We would track signup sources, remove invalid addresses, honor unsubscribe requests, and monitor engagement to maintain a healthy email list.
