01 / Join the list

You're invited.

Sign up for café news and a welcome offer. Just a few details and you're in.

Expect about 2 emails per month with menu highlights, café updates, and occasional offers. No purchased lists, no spam, and no selling your email address. Your subscription would only activate after you click a double opt-in confirmation in your inbox. Privacy details.

02 / Growing our community

Three ways to connect
with The Dot.

Our list-building plan meets guests where they already interact with the café. Every sign-up is voluntary, benefit-driven, and confirmed before receiving marketing emails.

01

In-store café guests

Where & how we collect

Place tabletop QR codes and a small counter sign at checkout, with staff inviting interested breakfast and lunch customers to join on their own phone.

Immediate value exchange

A welcome email offering a proposed 10% off their next visit once they confirm their subscription.

Long-term value promise

Occasional loyalty perks, menu updates, and first looks at seasonal favorites that give regulars a reason to return.

02

Iowa State students

Where & how we collect

Share a student-focused signup link through Instagram stories, campus-area flyers with QR codes, and student-created social posts.

Immediate value exchange

A proposed student welcome offer for a future café visit after confirming their email address.

Long-term value promise

Budget-friendly breakfast/lunch ideas, student-relevant updates, and occasional exclusive subscriber offers.

03

Online visitors & local followers

Where & how we collect

Feature this visible signup form on The Dot's website and link to it from organic social posts and café event announcements.

Immediate value exchange

Access to a proposed 10% welcome coupon by email after verifying the address.

Long-term value promise

Upcoming events, community news, menu additions, and occasional perks relevant to their interests.

Permission-first process: Each touchpoint links to a signup form with an unchecked consent box. A real email platform would send a confirmation message, retain the consent date/source, and add only verified subscribers to the marketing list. We would monitor engagement, honor unsubscribes, and remove hard-bounced addresses.
